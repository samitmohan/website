team has been working way too hard for the past,,, i dont even know how long. it was finally time for office off-site. with no laptopn in my bag i packed 3 pairs of shorts and made my way to our bus (a flight would've been better.. just saying)

---

## highlights

**day1:** bus ~ no alcohol with us (1 soju and 1 old monk with no coke doesn't count), it was time to prepare ourselves for goa. bus was mid except the fact that 2 of our office people were out like a light.. we had to stop in panjim itself instead of going to our stay in morjim cuz we were too impatient. plus i needed a beer first thing in the morning. it was a need. we went to [fisherman's wharf](https://www.instagram.com/thefishermanswharfpanjim/) for some food & drinks, nothing memorable. drove to our villa in morjim, picking up some beers, putting on some karan aujla and smoking with the cab driver who had a beautiful lighter and informational tips and tricks... we quickly left to rent scooties and went to [boiler maker](https://www.instagram.com/boilermaker.goa/?hl=en) immaculate, had great cocktails and bar snacks, our plan was to then go to [hyde](https://www.instagram.com/hideaway.goa/) and [juna](https://www.instagram.com/junagoa/) to end the night but duty calls, we had to go back for office team dinner...ugh. office dinner was at [tikiti](https://www.instagram.com/tikitii.goa/) which i kept calling tiki taka because lamine yamal is in my head at all timez.. it was off, the food was off, the fucking corona was off. only highlight was the washroom bcz that had more snow than a himachal. also the same 2 people got too drunk and kept blowing smoke onto other people's faces, they'd also gone swimming, walked into our senior's room naked, stole his wife's shirts and wore them to the dinner... at this point we were all just shocked but also laughing... mad respect to him for handling it well, i would've k*lled them. we were getting quite bored so we left without telling anyone (in pairs of two) but the CEO caught me leaving,,, he also saw me drink 2 beers and made me catwalk where he deliberately failed me even though my walk was more dainty than kate moss.. we went straight to juna since it was around 2 am, got 1 drink each and dipped... on our way back we got caught by the popos, pretended to pull over then hit the gear & ran, some f1 shit... got home, 6 of us upstairs for #debrief and mid debrief realised that the same two people left the house around 2 am without telling anyone, with no money, no phone charge, to drink more than they could possibly imagine. got a call around 3:30 am that they are out in some cafe and have no money to pay.. quickly rushed and realised one of them wasn't waking up at all, we had to rub his hands and feet with ice so he gets shocked.. didn't help. loaded him onto a scooty 3 idiots style and made sure he slept..

**day2:** woke up late, quickly showered & went to a deserted place to see goan culture, eat home grown vegetables, drink feni and jump from cliffs. great fun, almost died because i can't swim for shit & jumped from the cliff without a life jacket because.. i dont know actually.. need to introspect, worst part is i didnt even try to survive, i accepted my death and was just drowning, fortunately office people saved me. was in shock for the next hour or so. post swimming for 4 hours we had great goan mango curry and then went to [artjuna](https://www.instagram.com/artjunagoa/) cafe for some coffee & dessert with the team. had to cancel our drinking plans bcz others were too excited to go to [kokum curry](https://www.zomato.com/goa/kokum-curry-candolim) in candolim for some authentic goan food, so no drinks for us, the food was underwhelming despite having good reputation, we (the inner cirlce (4) left as early as we could, but it was 1 am and we decided to go to this 14 seater place called [room one](https://www.instagram.com/dropdeadsexygoa/?hl=en) and [drop dead sexy](https://www.instagram.com/dropdeadsexygoa/?hl=en), both were next to each other, had great great drinks there, watched the spain vs france match, our servers were cool and hated me because of my jersey (argentina).. we left around 3ish, got caught by the police again, i ran but the other two scooties were caught and we had to pay 500 rps, got people's lager and debriefed again till 5 am.

**day3:** up at 8 am, went to [ricemill](https://www.instagram.com/thericemill/?hl=en) (great vietnamese coffee) and left to see our junctions in goa.. great refresher course on electrical engineering, saw how to control traffic signal lights,, really fun,, everyone else left on the 2.1⭐ bus back to bangalore, we extended our trip for one (1) more day because a) we are not going by 2.1 ⭐ bus and b) 3 days is too less for goa... went to our friend's sister's house in panjim where we worked a little and then left for drinking time.. started at [kbar](https://www.instagram.com/kbargoa/) because i've heard only genz crowd is there, it was underwhelming, felt like 4s in delhi.. walked around a lot to find a place for the argentina england screening, found a place called [the pinch](https://www.instagram.com/pinchgoa/?hl=en) but it was horrible, it took them 2 hrs to get us some nachos, we got too upset and cornered the waiter, he kept running from us and was hiding in the bathroom... we got the bill and left in anger only to find a poor man's version of empire -> called [bawarchi restaurant](https://share.google/spG1keleej0lyREEh), had shawarma, watched the remaining of finals (argentina won), rolled cigarettes in under 10 seconds and finished all our drinks/smokes....

**day4:** woke up and quickly left for [slowtide](https://www.instagram.com/slowtidegoa/?hl=en) for some great cocktails by the ocean, 10/10.. phone died, nobody had a charger, no idea where our cab was parked.. had to use my company's data to find out the number of the owner based on his car number.. #proud employee moment.. post that we went to [burger factory](https://www.instagram.com/burgerfactorygoa/?hl=en) to eat this humongous yet scrumptious double decker bacon cheese burger and coconut cashew milkshake, 10/10 again... my manager wanted to visit this hostel called [the bucket list](https://www.instagram.com/bucketlisthostel/?hl=en) it was very homely and felt nice, played uno and left to buy some alcohol, got satiwa gin, kadamba goan whiskey and a few miniature bottles of cabo.. went to a few beaches, and lastly to little vagator beach where i found mr krishna who gave us a joint and also admitted to half murder,,, he also kept talking about the poha association and how much he loves his business of poha.. it was fun felt like i was back in gurgaon for an hour. left for the airport.. our friend and her sister were late for the flight and almost missed it, we kept stalling the airlines crew (7-star airlines) and finally post 20 mins of the final call they came running.. we asked for seats together and the cabin crew junaid put us in business class... i love you junaid. the flight was 1 hr 10 mins, it was a small plane and junaid ji didn't give a fuck about the flight, he kept flirting with the other business class person, they exchanged shayris (some letters to milena by kafka type shit) while we eavesdropped him shitting on his ex who was in air india and found out he's married and has a kid 4 yrs later.... we reached around 12 and went home because others were too scared to keep drinking.
